Privacy & source notices

CityLens is a local research and decision-support application. Imported city records may be incomplete. Report counts do not establish safety, physical conditions, or completed repairs.

What leaves this application

Your investigation text is sent to the configured language-model provider. User-supplied street addresses may be sent to the US Census and SanGIS public geocoders. If you explicitly search Google Places, your search expression is sent to Google. If configured, independent POI searches send your original expression to that separately licensed provider. Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information.

Saved conversations

When session storage is configured, questions, independently sourced or user-selected coordinates, selected Place IDs, evidence, and replies are saved in your configured PostgreSQL session store. Delete saved investigations from History. The browser stores only the selected session ID. Without persistent session storage, server memory is temporary.

Google Maps and Places

Use of Google Maps and Places is subject to the Google Maps Platform terms, the Google Maps additional terms, and the Google Privacy Policy. Google discovery names and addresses are displayed temporarily and are not copied into saved conversations. Google coordinates are not requested for analytical use. Google attribution and provider notices must remain visible.

Independent sources

City datasets: City of San Diego Open Data. Address resolution: US Census and SanGIS. The separate manual picker uses OpenStreetMap data and attribution. A user-selected point is not represented as a provider-verified address. Any separately configured independent POI provider has its own licensing and privacy requirements.

Local use

This interface has no production authentication or public-service operating controls. These notices describe the local implementation; a public deployment requires the operator’s own contact, retention, privacy, and service information.
